# Agent Quest — Rule Book

## Combat

Combat is turn-based. Each round, a combatant can take one action.

**Attack Roll:** Roll a d20. Add your strength modifier. A total of 15 or higher hits the target.

**Damage:** On a successful hit, roll the weapon's damage die:
- Fists: 1d4
- Rusty Sword: 1d6
- Battle Axe: 1d8
- Warhammer: 1d10

**Defense:** If attacked, the defender can attempt to dodge (d20, 15+ succeeds) to halve damage.

**Critical Hit:** A natural 20 on the attack roll deals double damage.
**Critical Miss:** A natural 1 on the attack roll means you drop your weapon or stumble.

## Skill Checks

When a player attempts something uncertain, they roll a d20 and add any relevant modifier.

**Difficulty Levels:**
- Easy: 10+
- Medium: 15+
- Hard: 18+
- Nearly Impossible: 20 (natural only)

**Common Skill Checks:**
- Persuasion: Convince an NPC (charisma-based)
- Stealth: Sneak past a guard or pick a pocket (dexterity-based)
- Perception: Notice a hidden trap or eavesdrop (wisdom-based)
- Athletics: Climb a wall, swim a river, arm wrestle (strength-based)
- Arcana: Identify a magical effect or decipher runes (intelligence-based)

## Magic

Spells consume spell slots. Once all slots are used, no more spells can be cast until resting.

**Available Spells:**
- **Fireball:** Deal 3d6 fire damage to a target. Costs 1 spell slot.
- **Heal:** Restore 2d6 HP to yourself or an ally. Costs 1 spell slot.
- **Shield:** Add +5 to your defense for the next attack against you. Costs 1 spell slot.
- **Lightning Bolt:** Deal 2d8 lightning damage to a target. Costs 1 spell slot.
- **Charm:** Attempt to charm an NPC (d20, 12+ succeeds). Costs 1 spell slot.

**Spell Failure:** If you cast a spell with 0 spell slots remaining, roll a d20. On a 1-10, the spell backfires and deals 1d6 damage to you.

**Resting:** A short rest (in-game) restores 1 spell slot. A long rest restores all spell slots and heals 1d6 HP.

## Tavern Activities

Taverns are social hubs. Here are some common activities:

**Ordering Drinks:**
- Ale: 1 gold. A solid drink.
- Fine Wine: 3 gold. Impressive, but pricey.
- Mystery Brew: 2 gold. Roll a d6: 1-2 you feel sick (-2 HP), 3-4 nothing happens, 5-6 you feel great (+2 HP).

**Arm Wrestling:**
- Both contestants roll d20 + strength modifier.
- Highest total wins. Ties are re-rolled.
- Standard wager: 5 gold coins.

**Gambling (Dice Game):**
- Both players roll 2d6. Closest to 12 without going over wins.
- Standard wager: 3 gold coins.

**Gathering Information:**
- Roll a d20 for persuasion. 12+ gets you a useful rumour. 18+ gets you a secret.
- Buying a round for the house (+5 gold) gives +3 to your persuasion roll.

## Resting and Recovery

**Short Rest:** Restore 1 spell slot. Takes about an hour in-game.
**Long Rest:** Restore all spell slots and heal 1d6 HP. Requires a safe place to sleep.
**Potion of Healing:** Restore 2d6 HP immediately. Can be purchased at shops for 15 gold.
